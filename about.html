Canadian Engineering

WORLD-CLASS
ENGINEERING ROOTS

EchoX was born from Canadian engineering excellence — a country renowned for its advanced materials science, precision manufacturing, and rigorous quality standards. Our founding team brought together decades of expertise from Canada's leading research institutions, with a singular mission: to create the world's most effective thermal protection film.

From our earliest days in Canadian laboratories, we recognized that existing window film solutions were designed for temperate climates and consistently failed under the relentless heat and UV intensity of the Arabian Gulf. This insight became our driving purpose — to engineer a product purpose-built for 50+ degree temperatures, intense solar radiation, and the harsh environmental conditions that define the GCC region.

Today, EchoX operates from our Qatar headquarters, serving the entire Gulf with premium thermal protection solutions. Our Canadian engineering DNA remains at the core of everything we do — from molecular-level material selection to precision coating processes, our engineering philosophy leaves nothing to chance.

Technology Overview

ADVANCED NANO THERMAL
PROTECTION TECHNOLOGY

Conventional window films rely on dyed layers that fade over time or metallic particles that block electronic signals. EchoX takes a fundamentally different approach. Our films incorporate nano-ceramic particles — materials engineered at the nanometer scale — that selectively absorb and reflect infrared heat while allowing visible light to pass through unaffected.

This proprietary technology is embedded within multiple layers of optical-grade polyester, each layer serving a specific function: UV absorption, infrared reflection, scratch resistance, and optical clarity. The result is a film that delivers superior thermal protection without compromising visibility, electronic device performance, or aesthetic quality.

Unlike metallic films that interfere with GPS, mobile, and satellite signals, our nano-ceramic construction is completely signal-transparent. And unlike dyed films that discolour and degrade under intense UV exposure, EchoX films maintain their optical clarity and thermal performance year after year, even in the harshest Gulf conditions.

SCRATCH-RESISTANT HARD COAT

The outermost layer protects against daily wear, cleaning, and environmental abrasion. Engineered to maintain optical clarity even after years of exposure to sand and dust.

OPTICAL-GRADE POLYESTER

The structural foundation of the film — ultra-clear polyester that provides dimensional stability and serves as the carrier for our functional nano-ceramic layers.

NANO-CERAMIC IR LAYER

The core thermal rejection layer. Nano-ceramic particles selectively absorb and reflect infrared radiation, blocking up to 95% of solar heat without affecting visible light transmission.

UV ABSORPTION LAYER

Dedicated UV-blocking layer that absorbs 99% of UVA and UVB radiation. Prevents skin damage, interior fading, and material degradation throughout the film's lifetime.

PRESSURE-SENSITIVE ADHESIVE

High-performance adhesive formulated for extreme heat resistance. Maintains bond integrity at 50°C+ without bubbling, peeling, or delaminating — guaranteed for 10 years.

RELEASE LINER

Protective liner removed during installation. Designed for clean separation without residue, ensuring the adhesive layer remains pristine for optimal glass adhesion.
